Add `Kbd` atomic — keyboard-key primitive. Styled `<kbd>` with monospace font, raised-key visual via subtle border, and three sizes (`sm | md | lg`) covering inline-prose usage, body-text default, and hero-callout overlays. Composes via children — `<Kbd>Cmd</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd>` — instead of a `combo` prop, matching native HTML semantics.
