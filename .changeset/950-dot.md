Add `Dot` atomic — single-color presence indicator. Renders a `<span>` decorative by default (`role="none"` + `aria-hidden="true"`); supply `label` to make it meaningful (`role="img"` + `aria-label`). Props: `color` (`neutral | primary | success | warning | danger | info`), `size` (`sm | md`, responsive), `status` (`online | offline | away | busy | idle`) as a sugar prop that emits `data-status` for status-driven styling. Supports `asChild` (Slot) for composition with custom elements.
